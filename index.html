Fifteen minutes above the city.
A world away.

Up the hill from Shandon, painted blue so you can't miss it, The Joshua Tree has been the Blarney Street local for nearly forty years — two doors, one long bar, and a beer garden out the back.

No airs, no graces. Just proper pints, the match on, a dartboard that's seen wars, and the kind of welcome you only get where everyone's a regular — even the first-timers.

The stout that lives here.

Murphy's has been brewed at Lady's Well, at the foot of this very hill, since 1856. Ours doesn't travel — it commutes. Look for the Murphy's sign over our door; it's been there longer than most of the regulars.
